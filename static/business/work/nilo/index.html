    Meet the shop

    Hello,we'renilo

    A tiny counter, a big copper churn and a crew who think gelato should taste like Cairo.

    Churned freshat sunrise

    Slow churn,
    fast love.

    Every batch starts before the city wakes up: whole milk from a farm in Fayoum, fruit from the Obour market and zero powdered shortcuts. We churn slowly so it stays dense and silky, serve it the same day, and never freeze yesterday's leftovers.

    Pull up a stool on the street, or have a pint at your door in thirty minutes.
