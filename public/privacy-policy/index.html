Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Foodidu. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe when you use foodidu.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you apply to become a partner, we collect the details you type into the form:

  • Business name and contact person
  • Phone number and email address
  • Business location, business type and website
  • Anything you write about your business

2.2 Usage information

  • Pages visited, time spent and the codes you copy
  • Device type, operating system, browser, screen size and language
  • Approximate location (country and city) estimated from your IP address
  • The page that referred you to Foodidu

2.3 Cookies and local storage

We store your cookie choice and a random visitor ID in your browser. Analytics cookies are only used after you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Understand which codes and pages are useful
  • Review partner applications and contact applicants
  • Send promotional offers and updates (with your consent)
  • Keep the platform secure and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Cloud Firestore): analytics and storing consent records, after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Microsoft Clarity: to understand how visitors use our pages, for example clicks and scrolling.
  • ipapi.co: to estimate your country and city from your IP address, after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: to receive partner applications.
  • Google Fonts: to load the fonts used on the site.

5. Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Partner restaurants and food delivery services
  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (listed above)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

6. Data security

We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data (the right to be forgotten)
  • Object to data processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email privacy@foodidu.com.

8. Cookie choices

You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

9. Third-party websites

Our pages link to brand websites and apps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies before sharing information with them.

10. Children’s privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post significant changes on this page.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email privacy@foodidu.com. Foodidu Privacy Team, Cairo, Egypt.
