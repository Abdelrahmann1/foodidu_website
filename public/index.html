    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    What is Foodidu?

    Foodidu is a free website that collects promo codes and discounts for restaurants, grocery apps and online shopping in Egypt and the GCC, in Arabic and English.

    Is Foodidu free?

    Yes. You don't need an account. Copy any code and use it directly in the brand's app or website.

    How do I use a promo code?

    Tap Copy on the code, open the brand's app or website, add your order to the cart and paste the code in the promo code field at checkout.

    Why didn't a code work?

    Most codes have conditions, such as first order only or a minimum order amount, and brands can change or end offers at any time. Check the conditions on the code page and make sure you typed it exactly.

    How can my restaurant appear on Foodidu?

    Fill in the partner application and our team will contact you.
