1. Introduction

Welcome to Foodidu. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe when you use foodidu.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you apply to become a partner, we collect the details you type into the form:

  • Business name and contact person
  • Phone number and email address
  • Business location, business type and website
  • Anything you write about your business

2.2 Usage information

  • Pages visited, time spent and the codes you copy
  • Device type, operating system, browser, screen size and language
  • Approximate location (country and city) estimated from your IP address
  • The page that referred you to Foodidu

2.3 Cookies and local storage

We store your cookie choice and a random visitor ID in your browser. Analytics cookies are only used after you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Understand which codes and pages are useful
  • Review partner applications and contact applicants
  • Send promotional offers and updates (with your consent)
  • Keep the platform secure and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Services we use

  • Google Analytics: visit and click statistics, after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): storing consent records after you accept analytics cookies, and partner applications you send us.
  • Microsoft Clarity: to understand how visitors use our pages, for example clicks and scrolling.
  • ipapi.co: to estimate your country and city from your IP address, after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: to receive partner applications.
  • Google Fonts: to load the fonts used on the site.

5. Push notifications and device identifiers (Foodidu app)

Push notifications. If you allow notifications, the Foodidu app registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) and receives a device token that lets us send you notices about new promo codes, deals and Foodidu Picks. The token identifies your device, not you; we do not link it to a name, email or phone number. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s Settings → Notifications → Foodidu, and the token is then no longer used. If you pick favourite food types in “My taste”, the app subscribes your device to topics for those types only (for example “sea food”) so that you receive relevant deals; no profile is stored on our servers.

Device identifiers and analytics. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config, which assign your installation an app-instance identifier and collect anonymous usage events (for example which Foodidu Pick was opened). These identifiers are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell or share them. Google processes this data under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Reviews and your anonymous account. To let you write, edit and delete your own reviews without creating an account, the app signs your device in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random user id that is stored with the reviews you write, together with the name and avatar you chose in the app. The id is not linked to your name, email or phone number, and only the device that wrote a review can edit or delete it; our team can remove reviews that are reported. Deleting the app ends this anonymous session.
